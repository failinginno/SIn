01 / LIFECYCLE

From entry
to entitlement.

01

Pool terms

Prize, ticket price, capacity, duration and fee are fixed when each pool is created. The first confirmed purchase starts its timer.

02

Ticket ledger

Each exact BNB payment records a numbered ticket and its purchasing wallet. A wallet's chance is its ticket count divided by the pool's capacity.

03

Draw or refund

When full, the pool requests a single VRF random word. If it expires before filling, buyers can claim their original contributions instead.

02 / FUNDS & AUTHORITY

The contract's
limits matter.

Participant funds

The deployed Manager has no administrator withdrawal route for active entries, winner prizes or refund liabilities. Contract defects remain possible; this is not an audit claim.

Creator

The fixed creator can create new pools. It cannot edit an existing pool, select a winner or reroll randomness.

Protocol fees

Fees accrue only after a successful settlement and are allocated to the separate Fee Vault. The fixed recipient can withdraw no more than recorded realized fees.

Wallet permissions

Connecting is not an ERC-20 approval. No project token is required. A purchase sends the exact native BNB amount shown in the wallet confirmation.

03 / RANDOMNESS

One request.
One recorded result.

A full pool requests one random word through its configured Chainlink VRF 2.5 provider. The winning zero-based ticket index is randomWord % capacity. The owner recorded for that ticket is the winner; the website cannot change the result.

VRF fulfillment requires the subscription and network infrastructure to function. An unfulfilled request can delay a draw. If no result arrives within six hours, buyers become eligible to claim their original BNB contributions; the refund still needs a wallet transaction and gas.

04 / REFUNDS

No settlement?
Contribution claim.

UNFILLED AT DEADLINE

No draw, no fee

An incomplete expired pool records each buyer's refundable contribution. The purchasing wallet claims it directly.

FULL, VRF UNFULFILLED

Six-hour timeout

After six hours without fulfillment, recorded buyers can claim their own contributions. A timed-out pool does not auto-create a replacement.
