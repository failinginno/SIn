REFUND PROTECTION

Not filled?
Your contribution stays claimable.

A pool draws only when every ticket sells. If the countdown reaches zero with tickets unsold, there is no winner and no protocol fee. Each buyer can claim back the full BNB they contributed.

  • No winner is selected
  • No protocol fee is collected
  • Each purchasing wallet claims its own BNB
Refund claims require a wallet transaction and network gas. A full pool with no VRF result after six hours also becomes refundable.

HOW IT WORKS

Five steps, no hidden pool terms. Read the price, prize, capacity and timer before buying.
